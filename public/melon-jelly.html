Inside the experiment

A volume filled with tetrahedra, solved with XPBD at 120 steps per second. Elastic edges, preserved cell volumes and a firmer rind let each part bend and recover. Contact friction and internal damping bring the wobble to rest.

Native WebGPU + WGSL. A back-face pass measures thickness for Beer–Lambert absorption, screen-space refraction, Fresnel reflections and soft transmitted light. Modeled seeds and air bubbles follow the same deforming volume. Shadows use a filtered light-space depth map.

Stylised optics and illustrative units, not a calibrated food model. Extreme pulls are limited to keep the mesh healthy. About XPBD ↗
