# Replacement koi asset research

Research checked 25 September 2026. Update 26 September: the user supplied a different model, **7PLUS Koi Fish**, with embedded CC BY 4.0 provenance. It is now installed as three morph-animated copies. See [integration notes](koi-integration.md) for its measured geometry, texture and animation limits. The candidates below are the earlier research, not installed assets. No purchase was made.

## Most promising open-license base

[Carp Fish by HuyKhoi2407](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/carp-fish-ef0c406d4ca84242a0464abecb7d7037), Sketchfab model `ef0c406d4ca84242a0464abecb7d7037`.

- Price: free on Sketchfab.
- The listing describes 4096 x 4096 maps, approximately 3,700 polygons and 0.37 m length. The public API reports 7,406 triangles, one animation and download availability.
- The public API's license is explicitly **CC BY 4.0**, with the canonical license URL. [The license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) allows redistribution and modification, including commercial use, with attribution, a license link and a notice of modifications. This permits the planned GLB delivery. Do not apply restrictions that remove recipients' CC rights.
- The inspected preview shows a realistic common carp with detailed scales, eyes, barbels and fins. It is **not already a Showa/Tancho/Ochiba koi pack**. Producing those varieties requires new coloration while retaining anatomical and scale detail.
- An animation is present, but the actual deformation bones, fin/gill tracks, embedded materials and seam quality have not been inspected. A preview and an animation count do not prove that the rig meets production acceptance.
- The official download endpoint returns HTTP 401 without authentication. Obtain the source download through a signed-in Sketchfab account. Do not extract viewer payloads or bypass authentication. Prefer the original Blender source if offered; otherwise request the downloadable glTF package with textures.
- Use the license of the acquired Sketchfab edition. The artist also offers products on other stores with different terms; the Creative Commons license is not automatically a license to every other edition or paid color pack.

Candidate attribution after actual use: “Carp Fish by HuyKhoi2407, CC BY 4.0, via Sketchfab. Modified for this aquarium: koi coloration, scale, material conversion, animation processing and mesh/texture optimization.” Include source and license links in the app credits and distributed asset notice, and revise the modification list to describe actual changes.

## Delivery status

Simulation, loading, independent skeletal/morph animation, retry/quality handling and underwater geometry rendering are implemented. The supplied 7PLUS model is installed; its low-detail morph animation does not meet the original high-detail skeletal requirement. Synthetic fish fixtures remain test-only and are never published as aquarium assets.
